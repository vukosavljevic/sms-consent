Solari Solari Storage

SMS Consent

Solari Storage collects explicit SMS consent before sending appointment-related text messages. Consent is never inferred from a phone number alone.

Customer SMS Consent

Customer consent is collected verbally during a phone call. The setter reads the full disclosure aloud and records whether the customer explicitly agreed or declined.

Declining SMS does not prevent appointment booking. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

  • Consent is collected during the setter intake / appointment booking flow.
  • The setter reads the exact disclosure below to the customer.
  • The setter selects Customer agreed or Customer declined — there is no pre-checked box.
  • Default state is not consented; a phone number alone does not imply consent.
  • Messages may include: appointment confirmations, day-before reminders, day-of reminders, rescheduling updates, and representative assignment / arrival updates.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Exact disclosure (read to customer)

Would you like to receive text messages from Solari Storage regarding your scheduled appointment, including appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling updates, and updates about your assigned representative? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

Example message to customer

Solari Storage: Reminder — your appointment is scheduled for today at [Time].
[Representative Name] is assigned to your appointment.
Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Representative SMS Consent

Authorized representatives (closers) opt in from the CRM under Settings → Notifications. The preference is off by default and must be actively enabled.

Representatives can disable SMS notifications at any time. SMS is optional and not required to use the CRM.

  • Location: Closer Dashboard → Settings → Notifications
  • Checkbox Receive appointment SMS notifications — OFF by default
  • Representative must actively check the box and click Save preference
  • Messages are operational only: appointment date/time, customer name, phone, address, notes, links
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Exact disclosure (shown in CRM settings)

I agree to receive operational SMS notifications from Solari Storage for appointments assigned to me. Messages may include appointment date and time, customer name, customer phone number, address, appointment notes, and appointment-related links. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. SMS notifications are optional.

Example message to representative

Solari Storage: Appointment assigned for [Date] at [Time].

Customer: [Customer Name]
Phone: [Customer Phone]
Address: [Address]
Language: [Language]
Agent: [Agent Name]
Recording: [Recording URL]
Notes: [Appointment Notes]

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
